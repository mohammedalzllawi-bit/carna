# Development Roadmap

## Phase 1 - MVP

- Auth, users, roles, refresh rotation.
- Dealers onboarding and approval.
- Vehicles with review workflow.
- Search and filters.
- Auctions and live bidding.
- Auction sessions.
- Admin dashboard foundation.
- Notifications.
- Deposit orders and payment adapter shell.
- Quick sale.
- Audit logs.

## Phase 2

- Technicians.
- Inspection booking and reports.
- Reviews.
- Complaints and disputes.
- Internal advertisements.
- Reports and exports.

## Phase 3

- Additional official payment providers.
- Advanced analytics.
- Advanced fraud detection.
- Advanced dealer subscriptions.
- AI-ready services: damage detection, price estimation, report summaries.
- Smart recommendations and semantic search.

## Non-Negotiable Tests

- simultaneous bids.
- auction ending and winner calculation.
- reserve price.
- deposit calculation.
- duplicate payment webhook.
- failed payment.
- anti-sniping extension.
- blocked user cannot bid.
- owner/dealer cannot bid on own vehicle.
